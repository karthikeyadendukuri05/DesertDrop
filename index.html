What makes this decision smarter?

🌱 Detect actual need
Each crop has a sample moisture threshold. If soil is already moist enough, skip watering entirely.
📊 Estimate liters, not just a warning
Use the deficit to a target moisture, bed area, estimated root depth, wetting fraction, and irrigation efficiency.
🚰 Protect the water budget
Prioritize thirstiest beds first. Never recommend more water than is available.
☀️ Respect desert constraints
Show heat, humidity, and salinity warnings; recommend cooler irrigation hours.

Prototype assumptions: Thresholds, target moisture, rooting depth and drip wetting fraction are illustrative engineering assumptions for demonstration, not crop-specific field recommendations. Actual irrigation requires calibrated sensors, soil texture, crop stage, evapotranspiration, salinity management and local agronomic review. Water in the model is calculated from a simplified root-zone deficit with 25% wetted area and 90% irrigation application efficiency. All calculations happen locally in this browser; no data is uploaded. Red sea · Breaking Bytes hackathon.
